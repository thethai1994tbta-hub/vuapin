  (This deploys your app - wait for the build to finish)

To send order confirmation emails:

1. Go to https://myaccount.google.com
2. Click "Security" on the left
3. Enable "2-Step Verification" if not already enabled
4. Search for "App passwords" (below 2-Step Verification)
5. Select "Mail" and "Windows Computer"
6. Google generates a 16-character password
7. Use THIS password for EMAIL_PASSWORD (not your Gmail password!)
